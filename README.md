One original one-line joke about office life: the morning the office coffee machine broke, and by 10am the whole floor had accidentally done three hours of real work.

> The office coffee machine broke this morning, and by 10am the whole floor had accidentally done three hours of work.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the joke to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 200 characters of UTF-8 text, a single line.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
